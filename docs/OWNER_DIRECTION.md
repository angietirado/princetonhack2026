# Regulars: third approach

## Product decision
The owner brings a store's sales history and asks: Who is returning, which patterns changed, and what should I test next? Start with independent cafes and shops rather than a multi-industry CRM. The product pairs understandable arithmetic with an evidence-backed investigation and a small, measurable owner action.

Working demo: Harbor & Pine, a fictional cafe. The synthetic dataset is explicitly marked and anchored in time. It must never be presented as real bank-derived customer intelligence.

## Retain
- Docker Compose, nginx, nonroot/read-only containers and bounded resources.
- FastAPI, Pydantic, locked dependencies, integer-cent arithmetic and explicit dates.
- Safe rendering, request cancellation, memory-only tokens and no-store responses.
- Optional model/provider integration patterns and regression infrastructure.

## Replace in the active application
- Personal accounts, balances, spending categories and recurring subscriptions with merchant sales, observed customers, cohorts and experiments.
- One pinned consumer's Nessie data with business-scoped sales records. Personal banking access does not authorize merchant surveillance.
- Housing-search controls with customer evidence and practical owner decisions.
- Legacy settings that automatically read .env with explicit injected owner configuration.

Legacy modules and tests stay in this initial fork for reference; Docker runs only the merchant entry point and merchant test suite. Separate names, ports and volumes prevent collisions with approaches one and two. No original repository files are changed.

## Analytics contract
All monetary arithmetic uses cents. Reporting dates are inclusive UTC dates. First observed purchase uses all available history through the selected end, not just the reporting period. Returning share is active identified customers first observed before the period divided by active identified customers. Anonymous sales do not become one artificial customer. Refund accounting, segment rules, coverage and cohort maturity must remain visible.

## Current implementation
The owner dashboard, reviewed CSV import, immutable dataset selection, exact revenue comparison and shared experiment budget calculator are implemented. Optional Deep Agents uses aggregate tools with bounded calls and no customer records. Optional Nessie import reads one explicitly selected sandbox merchant and saves synthetic provenance; live credentials have not been tested.

## Next iterations
Continue finding and fixing concrete owner-workflow problems, keep the runnable demo current, and improve explanations for incomplete history. A real business consultation would supply the next high-value product evidence. Sponsor eligibility, live provider verification and eventual publication remain separate from offline implementation.

Business + Enterprise is the natural main-track candidate. LangChain Deep Agents and Amazon agentic AI fit investigation and adaptive owner planning. Capital One Nessie could supply a clearly synthetic merchant sales scenario or business cash context; it does not establish consent or access to all customers' banking. Peri depends on actual business/club consultation, which is not currently supplied. Photon could deliver an owner-approved digest; no messages are sent by this prototype. CoStar requires substantive spatial work and should not be claimed from a token listing lookup. Detailed research follows as verified.
