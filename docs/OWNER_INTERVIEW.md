# Validate Regulars with an actual owner

No consultation has been conducted or invented for this fork. This short guide is for the user to conduct one; the app should follow the owner's actual problem rather than use the conversation as a prize checkbox.

## A focused conversation

1. When you say business is going well or badly, what do you look at first? Ask for a recent decision, not a hypothetical feature wish list.
2. What question about customers could you not answer from your current sales tools? Examples to explore only after their answer: repeat visits, quiet regulars, refunds, or a change in customer mix.
3. What does a useful next action look like for you? Ask about staff time, offer budget, who can be contacted, and a reasonable evaluation window.
4. How are customers identified in your records? Establish whether IDs are stable, whether anonymous orders exist, and what dates/statuses/refunds the export includes. Regulars currently requires pseudonymous IDs and explicit UTC dates.
5. Show a small workflow using fictional data first. Ask the owner to explain the numbers back in their own words, find a customer's evidence, and decide whether a proposed pilot is worth reviewing.

## Record the useful evidence

Capture the business or qualifying organization name, consulted person's name and role, date, the concrete problem they identified, the feedback that changed the product, and the evidence they agree may be included in a submission. Do not invent endorsements or treat generic playtesting as a business consultation.

Use a short before/after statement: "The owner could not answer [specific question]. We changed [specific workflow] so they could inspect [evidence] and decide [action]." If their feedback contradicts the cafe persona or the current retention focus, update the product direction.

Only describe a real consultation in the Peri submission after it has actually happened. This document is preparation, not eligibility evidence.
